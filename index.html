Our Services

We offer a wide range of security and IT solutions tailored to your needs

CCTV Camera Installation

Professional installation of high-definition CCTV systems for homes and businesses. We provide both indoor and outdoor cameras with remote monitoring capabilities, ensuring 24/7 surveillance and peace of mind.

Access Control Systems

Advanced access control solutions including biometric scanners, key card systems, and digital locks. We help you manage who enters your premises with customizable security levels and audit trails.

Electric Fencing

Installation of perimeter electric fencing systems that provide a strong deterrent against intruders. Our systems are safe, reliable, and designed to meet specific security requirements for any property.

Intruder Alarm Systems

Comprehensive intruder detection systems with sensors, alarms, and direct connection to security response teams. Our systems are designed to detect unauthorized entry and alert you immediately.

Intercom Systems

Installation of modern intercom systems for residential and commercial buildings. Our solutions include video intercoms, door entry systems, and integration with access control for seamless communication.

Networking & Structure Cabling

Professional networking solutions including LAN/WAN setup, WiFi installation, and structured cabling for data, voice, and video. We ensure reliable connectivity for homes and businesses of all sizes.

General IT Support

Comprehensive IT support services including hardware/software troubleshooting, system maintenance, virus removal, data backup solutions, and IT consultancy for businesses in Nairobi.
